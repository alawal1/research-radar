# This week in AI governance: 4 new papers (1 peer-reviewed, 2 preprints, 1 other).

## TLDR
- Grassroots contracts could significantly mitigate AI risks for digital platforms.
- A hybrid model for AI governance in Uzbekistan may enhance public sector efficiency.
- Understanding AI transparency is crucial for increased opt-in rates in usage-based insurance.

## AI risk & liability
**Corralling AI Risks with Grassroots Contracts** [preprint]  
The paper proposes grassroots contracts as a novel approach to address and mitigate major AI risks associated with digital platforms.  

**AI Transparency and Usage-Based Insurance Opt-In Rates: A Verify-First Guide** [preprint]  
This paper provides a foundational guide on enhancing AI transparency, emphasizing its importance in improving opt-in rates within usage-based insurance systems.

## Public sector AI
**DAVLAT XIZMATLARIDA SUN’IY INTELLEKTNI HUQUQIY TARTIBGA SOLISH: YEVROPA ITTIFOQI, AQSH VA XITOY MODELLARINING QIYOSIY TAHLILI VA O‘ZBEKISTON UCHUN XULOSALAR** [peer-reviewed]  
The study analyzes and compares different AI governance models in public services from Europe, the US, and China, proposing a hybrid governance model tailored for Uzbekistan.

## Other
**Artificial Intelligence, Data Governance and International Trade Law: A Review of Legal Challenges, Facilitation Technologies and Governance Pathways** [other]  
This paper reviews the legal challenges surrounding AI in international trade and outlines possible governance pathways to improve regulatory frameworks.
