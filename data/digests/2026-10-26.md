# This week in AI governance: 8 new papers (0 peer-reviewed, 6 preprints, 2 other).

## TLDR
- A new framework integrates AI governance with emotional computing, providing a holistic approach to AI regulation.
- Mexico's AI agenda is addressing key risks related to sovereignty amid global governance challenges.
- The EU's AI regulatory landscape reveals significant gaps that could impact compliance for companies.

## AI risk & liability
**THE TRUSTWORTHY INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK FROM DEEP LEARNING TO EMOTIONAL AI AND GOVERNANCE** [preprint]  
This paper offers a comprehensive framework that combines AI governance with emotional computing and legal considerations for effective global regulation.  
[Read more](https://openalex.org/W7222950320)

**Human–AI Interaction and System Usability: A Risk-Calibrated Computational Framework for AI Liability** [other]  
The paper proposes a framework that emphasizes the role of system usability in determining accountability mechanisms for AI liability.  
[Read more](https://openalex.org/W7222994015)

**AI Risks and Sovereignty: What Mexico's Institutional Agenda Recognises and Addresses** [preprint]  
Mexico's institutional agenda for AI identifies critical risks while addressing sovereignty concerns, suggesting a proactive approach amidst global challenges.  
[Read more](https://openalex.org/W7222944247)

**Is Your Data Ready for AI? What Five Years of Reports from 26 Regulators Reveal About Data and AI Governance** [preprint]  
This research evaluates the commitments of health regulators in Ontario regarding data governance in AI over a five-year period, highlighting regulatory directions.  
[Read more](https://openalex.org/W7223466783)
