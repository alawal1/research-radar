### AI risk & liability
**AI Governance Policy Dataset: Shadow AI Risk and Acceptable Use Policy Decisions for 11,890 Generative AI Tools across 15 Sector** [other]  
This dataset sheds light on the varied acceptable use policies for generative AI tools, helping organizations understand compliance risks.

**AI Acceptable Use Policy Benchmark: How 15 Sectors Govern 11,890 Generative AI Tools (Shadow AI Governance Statistics, 2026)** [preprint]  
This paper benchmarks how different sectors manage generative AI tools, revealing significant differences in policy rigor.

**Secure Human Oversight of AI: Threat Modeling in a Socio-Technical Context** [other]  
This study investigates security risks associated with human oversight in AI and offers strategies for risk mitigation.
