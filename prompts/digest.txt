You are writing a short weekly newsletter about new AI governance research.

The reader is a consultant who advises companies on AI governance. They want to know what's new, what matters, and what to do with it. They have 3 minutes to read this.

--- WHAT YOU KNOW ---

Date: {date}
Total new papers this week: {total_count}
Peer-reviewed: {peer_reviewed_count}
Preprints: {preprint_count}

Papers to cover (each has a title, type, theme, summary, and URL):

{papers_list}

--- FORMAT ---

Write a 500–700 word markdown newsletter with this exact structure:

1. HEADER (one line)
   Format: "This week in AI governance: {total_count} new papers ({peer_reviewed_count} peer-reviewed, {preprint_count} preprints)."

2. TLDR (up to 3 bullets)
   Pick the most important or interesting items from the list. One sentence each. Not a summary — a takeaway a consultant can use.
   If there are fewer than 3 papers, use one bullet per paper. Do not split one paper into multiple bullets.

3. GROUPED BY THEME
   Group the papers by their theme.    Use the theme field from each paper to group them. Do not invent new theme names.
    For each theme, a subheading, then one short paragraph per paper.
   Each paper entry:
   - **Title** [peer-reviewed or preprint]
   - one_line_summary (or your own version if you can improve it)
   
   Order themes by how many papers they have (most first).
   If a theme has only one paper and it's minor, put it in an "Other" section at the bottom.

4. CLOSING LINE
   One short sentence. Something like "See the sheet for the top 5 this week." Keep it simple.

--- RULES ---

- Do not invent papers. Only use what's in the list.
- Do not add your own opinions about the field.
- Do not write an introduction or conclusion beyond the structure above.
- Keep paragraphs short. The reader is skimming.
- Use plain language. No jargon unless it's in a paper title.

--- OUTPUT FORMAT ---

Return only the markdown newsletter. No JSON, no preamble.
