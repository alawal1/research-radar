You are extracting structured information from one academic paper about AI governance.

You will be given a paper's title, metadata, and abstract. You will return a single JSON object with three parts:

1. FEASIBILITY SCORES — four scores, each 0–2
2. DIGEST HELPERS — a theme label and a one-line summary
3. SHEET FIELDS — sixteen text fields for the research database

--- PART 1: FEASIBILITY SCORES ---

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2:
- 0 = not present
- 1 = partially or weakly present
- 2 = clearly present

The four criteria:

1. relevant — Is the paper clearly about AI governance?
2. positioned — Does the paper discuss and position itself against prior work?
3. practical — Does the paper have real-world implications?
4. rigorous — Is the paper scientifically sound?

For rigorous, do not equate "not empirical" with "unrigorous." A conceptual paper with a clear, coherent argument and a proposed framework can score 1, even without data or experiments. Score 0 only if the abstract is incoherent, unsupported, or vague.

--- PART 2: DIGEST HELPERS ---

theme — pick exactly one from this list:
- EU regulation
- Global standards
- Corporate compliance
- AI risk & liability
- Auditing & certification
- Public sector AI
- Other

one_line_summary — one sentence (max 25 words) that a consultant would find useful. Not a description of the paper. A takeaway.

--- PART 3: SHEET FIELDS ---

Extract these sixteen fields from the abstract. If the abstract does not state something, write exactly: "Not stated in abstract"

- insights — key insights derived from the paper
- conclusions — main conclusions drawn by the authors
- methods — methodologies, frameworks, or techniques used
- limitations — limitations or gaps acknowledged
- contributions — unique contributions to the field
- summary_abstract — a short summary of the abstract
- results — main findings and results reported
- literature_survey — previous research discussed and its relevance
- practical_implications — real-world applications mentioned
- objectives — research objectives stated by the authors
- findings — concise summary of the findings
- research_gap — the research gap identified or inferred
- future_research — suggested future research directions
- dataset — datasets used, including sources
- challenges — challenges or difficulties noted
- applications — applications discussed

Important:
- Use only information from the abstract. Do not add outside knowledge.
- If a field is partially addressed, write what is stated, not what you assume.
- "Not stated in abstract" is a complete and acceptable answer. Do not guess.

Return ONLY valid JSON. No prose, no markdown fences.
